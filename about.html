About MED ASIDE

The team behind your clients' compliance and long-term security.

Who We Are

Built for Professionals Like You

MED ASIDE was founded by settlement administration veterans who spent years watching attorneys and adjusters struggle with impersonal, bureaucratic MSA providers. Cases fell through the cracks. Compliance gaps exposed clients. Deadlines were missed.

We built MED ASIDE to be the firm you actually want to refer to — responsive, precise, and accountable. Every case gets a dedicated specialist. Every filing is airtight. Every referral is acknowledged within 24 hours.

Our team is composed of Medicare compliance specialists, certified trust administrators, and lien resolution experts who understand the nuances of CMS requirements across workers' compensation, liability, and no-fault settlements.
