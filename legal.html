HIPAA & Protected Health Information Notice

Last updated: March 2026

5.1 Receipt of Protected Health Information

In the course of providing Medicare Set-Aside administration, lien resolution, settlement consulting, and related services, MED ASIDE may receive Protected Health Information (PHI) as defined under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 (HIPAA) and its implementing regulations. PHI may include, but is not limited to:

  • Medical records, treatment notes, and diagnostic reports.
  • Prescription histories and pharmaceutical records.
  • Physician and provider information.
  • Medicare claim histories and billing information.
  • Health Insurance Claim Numbers (HICNs) and Medicare Beneficiary Identifiers (MBIs).
  • Any individually identifiable health information received in connection with case administration.

5.2 Safeguards

MED ASIDE maintains comprehensive administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect PHI, including:

  • Administrative safeguards: Designated privacy and security personnel, workforce training on PHI handling, policies and procedures governing access, use, and disclosure of PHI, and regular risk assessments.
  • Technical safeguards: Encryption of PHI in transit and at rest, secure access controls with unique user identification and authentication, audit logging and monitoring of access to systems containing PHI, and automatic session timeouts.
  • Physical safeguards: Restricted access to facilities and workstations where PHI is processed or stored, secure disposal of physical and electronic media containing PHI, and environmental controls for data storage infrastructure.

5.3 Use of PHI

PHI received by MED ASIDE is used exclusively for purposes directly related to case administration, including:

  • Preparing and reviewing Medicare Set-Aside allocations.
  • Submitting MSA proposals to CMS for review and approval.
  • Administering MSA accounts and processing medical payments.
  • Resolving Medicare conditional payment and other healthcare liens.
  • Communicating with CMS, attorneys, and healthcare providers as necessary for case administration.
  • Preparing annual attestations and compliance reports required by CMS.

MED ASIDE does not use PHI for marketing, advertising, or any purpose unrelated to case administration and service delivery.

5.4 Disclosure of PHI

MED ASIDE discloses PHI only as necessary for case administration and only to the following parties:

  • Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS): As required for MSA review, approval, attestation, and compliance.
  • Treating physicians and healthcare providers: As necessary to verify treatment, obtain medical records, or coordinate care within the scope of MSA administration.
  • Attorneys of record: Attorneys representing the claimant/injured party, as authorized and necessary for case coordination.
  • Insurance carriers and adjusters: As authorized and necessary for settlement administration and lien resolution.
  • As required by law: In response to valid legal process, court orders, or as otherwise required by applicable federal or state law.

PHI is never disclosed for purposes unrelated to case administration, and all disclosures are limited to the minimum necessary information required to accomplish the intended purpose.

5.5 Covered Entity Status

Important clarification: MED ASIDE is not a covered entity as defined under HIPAA (i.e., we are not a health plan, healthcare clearinghouse, or healthcare provider that conducts standard electronic transactions). However, as a matter of professional responsibility and best practice, MED ASIDE voluntarily maintains HIPAA-level protections for all PHI we receive. We handle all health information as if we were subject to the full requirements of the HIPAA Privacy Rule and Security Rule, because we believe this is the appropriate standard of care for the sensitive information entrusted to us.

Where MED ASIDE operates as a Business Associate of a covered entity, we will enter into a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) as required by HIPAA.

5.6 Breach Notification

In the unlikely event of a breach of unsecured PHI, MED ASIDE is committed to the following:

  • Prompt investigation: We will promptly investigate any suspected or confirmed breach of PHI.
  • Notification: We will notify affected individuals and, where applicable, the covered entity with whom we have a Business Associate Agreement, without unreasonable delay and in no event later than sixty (60) calendar days after discovery of the breach.
  • Content of notice: Notification will include a description of the breach, the types of information involved, steps individuals should take to protect themselves, what MED ASIDE is doing to investigate and mitigate the breach, and contact information for further inquiries.
  • Regulatory reporting: We will report breaches to the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services (HHS) and applicable state attorneys general as required by law.
  • Mitigation: We will take reasonable steps to mitigate harmful effects of the breach.

5.7 Your Rights Regarding PHI

While MED ASIDE may not be a HIPAA-covered entity in all contexts, we respect the spirit of HIPAA's individual rights provisions. If you are a claimant or injured party whose PHI we have received, you may:

  • Request access to the PHI we hold about you.
  • Request correction of PHI you believe to be inaccurate.
  • Request an accounting of disclosures of your PHI.
  • Request restrictions on certain uses or disclosures (subject to our operational needs and legal obligations).

To exercise these rights, please contact us at info@medaside.com or by mail at MED ASIDE, PO Box 8294, Van Nuys, CA 91410.
